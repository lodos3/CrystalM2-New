Brackeys' VFX Bundle (CC0)
https://brackeysgames.itch.io/brackeys-vfx-bundle

The flame, fire and smoke particle masks come from the flipbooks by Thomas Iché.
Source sheets: flame_01_16x4.tga, fire_03_8x8.tga and wispy_smoke_01_8x8.tga.
Each clip retains 24 evenly spaced frames at the source frame resolution. The
client stores them as single-channel coverage so each spell can tint them. The
upright flame has a lifted plume and a feathered base for the isometric floor.
https://unity.com/blog/engine-platform/free-vfx-image-sequences-flipbooks

The Warrior crescent, triple cut, spark, dust and shock ring masks are adapted
from the CC0 particle textures collected in Brackeys' bundle. Source files:
slash_03_a.png, scratch_01_a.png, star_07_a.png, dirt_01_a.png, circle_03_a.png.
The bundle credits Picster and Kenney for its particle textures.

Free Hit VFX — Slash Crimson by berryhibiscus
https://berryhibiscus.itch.io/hit-vfx-pack-30-stylized-impact-effects-for-godot-4

The Warrior hit mask is adapted from t_stylizedhit_1.png from FreeHitVfx.zip.
The sample allows modification and use in personal and commercial projects.
It restricts redistribution of the files as a standalone asset pack.

ARCFLUX Lite
https://gennai.itch.io/arcflux-lite

The Wizard lightning paths adapt the midpoint displacement, branch taper and
halo smoothing from ARCFLUX Lite's arcflux_path.gd to the game's 2D world.
